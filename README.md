# licheeeens

**Status: Planned**

Lichen species, their traits and their growth surfaces are planned for eeeearth local scenes. Implementation has not started.

Mosses and liverworts belong to a separate planned bryophyte module. Biological soil crusts are community relationships in `seeeeds`, with soil effects in `deeeecomp`.

Learn the science in the [Earth systems guide](https://github.com/eeeearth/eeeearth/tree/main/docs/earth-systems), or watch the [live stream](https://www.youtube.com/@jt55401/live). The simulation covers local scenes and does not model every Earth system.
